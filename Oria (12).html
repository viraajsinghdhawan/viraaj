How it works

You don't need a plan yet

Oria is for the stage before you pick a major. Three steps get you from "I'm not sure" to a short list of real questions.

  1. STEP 1

    Browse or swipe

    Search by subject, interest or career, filter by study area, or flip through cards one at a time.

  2. STEP 2

    Save what's interesting

    Tap the heart on any path. Your shortlist stays in this browser and is never sent anywhere.

  3. STEP 3

    Ask an advisor

    Each saved path comes with questions about classes, transfer, admission and cost for your next conversation.

    Questions

    Before you rely on this

    Is Oria an official NWACC tool?

    No. Oria is a student project prototype. The study paths and career examples are illustrative and are not a verified list of NWACC programs. Check official NWACC program, transfer and advising information before making academic decisions.

    Where are my saved paths stored?

    Only in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent to a server, so clearing your site data or switching browsers will empty the list. Use Copy my shortlist if you want to keep it.

    What do the path labels mean?

    Transfer pathways are meant to be continued at a four-year school. Career and technical pathways lean toward workforce credentials. Selective programs have their own admission process. These labels are broad guides and do not confirm the exact credential offered today.

    Does saving a path mean I've chosen a major?

    No. Saving only marks something you're curious about. Use the questions in your shortlist to learn what the program actually involves.

    Where can I check current programs and requirements?

    Start with the NWACC areas of study and degree catalog pages linked in the footer, then confirm details with an NWACC advisor.
